# Privacy Policy for ZenArchieve

**Last Updated:** October 2026

ZenArchieve ("we", "our", or "the application") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information and data security.

---

### 1. Zero Data Collection
ZenArchieve is a desktop archive manager designed to operate locally on your computer. 
- **We do NOT collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data.**
- We do not require account creation, registration, or login.
- We do not collect telemetry, analytics, usage statistics, or user behavior tracking.

---

### 2. File and Archive Processing
- All compression, decompression, hashing (SHA-256), encryption, and preview operations take place **strictly locally on your device**.
- No files, archive contents, passwords, file names, or folder paths are ever uploaded or transmitted to any remote servers.

---

### 3. Network and Online Communication
ZenArchieve connects to the internet solely for the following optional feature:
- **Online Update Checks:** The application queries the public GitHub Releases API (`https://api.github.com/repos/Tabish955/ZenArchieve/releases/latest`) to check if a newer version is available.
- This request contains only standard HTTP headers and does not transmit any personal information, device identifiers, or user files.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services and Advertising
- ZenArchieve is 100% free, open-source, and contains **zero advertisements, bundleware, or third-party trackers**.

---

### 5. Open Source Transparency
ZenArchieve is open-source software under the MIT License. The full source code is publicly accessible and auditable at:
https://github.com/Tabish955/ZenArchieve

---

### 6. Contact
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or ZenArchieve, please contact us via our official repository:
https://github.com/Tabish955/ZenArchieve/issues
